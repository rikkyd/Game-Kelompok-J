### Scene 1:  
On a bright sunny day in the Kingdom of Amarta, Wara Sembadra sat alone in the palace garden.  

She hummed softly, her voice like the gentle breeze bringing tranquility.  

### Scene 2:  
Unbeknownst to her, hidden behind the dense, dark trees, a pair of sharp eyes watched her intently.  

Burisrawa secretly observed her from afar.  

### Scene 3:  
Burisrawa stepped out from his hiding place. Sembadra was startled and immediately stood up, her eyes filled with fear.  

"Burisrawa, please leave me alone. I am already married," she said softly, asserting that her heart would never waver.  

### Scene 4:  
Without warning, Burisrawa drew his dagger and pointed it directly at Sembadra's face.  

"If you keep refusing, then suffer the consequences!" he threatened. But Sembadra stood firm, though fear began to consume her.  

### Scene 5:  
"Rather than surrendering my body to disgrace and betrayal, I would rather die defending my honor and loyalty to my husband!"  

Sembadra swiftly plunged the dagger into her chest.  

### Scene 6:  
From a distance, Srikandi was startled by Sembadra's heart-wrenching scream. Filled with anxiety, she ran toward the source of the sound.  

### Scene 7:  
Burisrawa was shocked to see Sembadra's actions.  

Fear and guilt immediately overwhelmed him.  

### Scene 8:  
Without thinking twice, Burisrawa fled the scene, leaving Sembadra's lifeless body lying in the garden, blood beginning to flow.  

### Scene 9:  
Srikandi was horrified to find Sembadra lying lifeless, blood streaming from the wound on her chest.  

### Scene 10:  
But there was no time for grief.  

Srikandi heard hurried footsteps coming from the dark forest.  

Her sharp eyes fixed on the source of the sound, full of caution.  

### Scene 11:  
Srikandi quickly ran toward the forest, following the sound of the retreating footsteps.  

However, when she arrived, she saw no one.  

Unwilling to give up, she pressed on, running through the darkness of the trees with a burning determination to find the perpetrator who had taken Sembadra's life.
